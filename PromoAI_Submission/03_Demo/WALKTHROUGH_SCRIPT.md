# PromoAI — 2-Minute Video Walkthrough Script
**Live Application URL:** https://promoai11.streamlit.app/
**Author:** Shib Chandan Mistry

---

### Segment 1: Introduction & Problem Context (0:00 - 0:25)
* **Visual:** Open browser to `https://promoai11.streamlit.app/`. Show the dark editorial studio masthead.
* **Voiceover:**
  > "Hello! This is PromoAI, an automated promotional moment discovery and editorial ranking studio built for the Kuku AI Generalist assignment.
  > In serialized vertical microdramas, finding high-converting 20 to 60-second promo moments across hundreds of script pages is a major bottleneck.
  > PromoAI uses a neuro-symbolic architecture combining Google Gemini AI with a deterministic 5-factor scoring formula to automate this end-to-end."

---

### Segment 2: Script Ingestion & Hollywood Reader (0:25 - 0:50)
* **Visual:** On Home Studio, click **'Load 5 episodes (Ep 1–5)'**. The manifest table populates with 5 valid files. Then navigate to **Screenplay Reader**.
* **Voiceover:**
  > "Here on Home Studio, we can ingest episode TXT or ZIP archives. Let's load 5 pre-packaged sample episodes.
  > Navigating to the Screenplay Reader, our parser extracts formatted Hollywood sluglines, character dialogue, parenthetical emotions, and computes a 10-point scene intensity curve for every scene."

---

### Segment 3: Candidate Generation & 9:16 Video Monitor (0:50 - 1:30)
* **Visual:** Switch to **Promo Studio**, select strategy, and click **'Generate promo candidates'**. When generated, jump to **Ranked Candidates**.
* **Voiceover:**
  > "In Promo Studio, we discover candidates balancing single-episode confrontations and cross-episode narrative arcs.
  > Over in Ranked Candidates, cuts are ranked by our deterministic formula: S = 0.25H + 0.20E + 0.25C + 0.15K + 0.15P.
  > Notice the interactive 9:16 Video Cutting Monitor on the right: it displays the mobile aspect ratio, TikTok/Reels social safe-zone boundaries, dynamic pacing timeline bars (0–3s hook, body, cliffhanger cut), and real-time burned-in subtitles."

---

### Segment 4: Review & Export Hub (1:30 - 2:00)
* **Visual:** Go to **Editorial Review**, click Approve on Candidate #1. Then navigate to **Export Hub** and show the JSON preview.
* **Voiceover:**
  > "In Editorial Review, marketing directors can approve cuts, tweak hook wording, and leave directions for video editors.
  > Finally, in Export Hub, we download the production-ready standardized JSON schema, CSV summaries, and executive Markdown reports.
  > The entire system is backed by a 44-test automated test suite with 100% pass rate. Thank you!"

---

### Fast Screen Recording Guide (Windows):
1. Open Chrome to `https://promoai11.streamlit.app/`.
2. Press `Win + Alt + R` (built-in Windows Game Bar screen recorder) or open Loom.
3. Walk through the 4 steps above following this script.
4. Replace `PromoAI_Walkthrough.mp4` in this folder with your finalized recording if desired.
